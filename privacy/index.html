Privacy policy

For sevenlanternsmusic.com and the Seven Lanterns Stats app. Last updated 22 November 2026.

Who is responsible

In short

This website sets no cookies, shows no ads and does not track you across sites. We only process personal data when you choose to give it to us (by joining the mailing list or writing to us).

What we process and why

Visiting the siteThe site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). To deliver pages, GitHub's servers receive your IP address and browser details and keep them briefly in security logs. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Transfers to the USA rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
Mailing list & free guidesIf you request a guide or subscribe, we store your first name, email address, the guide you chose and the date of your consent with our email service MailerLite (UAB MailerLite, Lithuania, EU), which processes it on our behalf. We use it to send the guide and the Lantern Letter (about once a month). Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Every email has an unsubscribe link; after you unsubscribe we keep only what is needed to respect that choice.
Emails and enquiriesIf you email us (for example through the licensing form, which only opens your own email app), we use your message to reply and, if you buy a licence, to issue the agreement and invoice. Legal basis: steps before a contract / the contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) and legal obligations such as keeping invoices (Art. 6(1)(c)). Invoices are kept for the period required by Spanish tax law; other correspondence for up to two years.
YouTube videosVideos are not loaded until you press play. Only then does your browser connect to YouTube (Google) via youtube-nocookie.com, and Google's privacy policy applies.

We never sell your data and never use it for advertising.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its use, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time, by emailing info@sevenlanternsmusic.com. If you think we have not handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the AEPD, or the authority in your own EU country.

Seven Lanterns Stats (Google / YouTube data)

Seven Lanterns Stats is an internal tool used only by the Seven Lanterns team to view statistics of our own YouTube channels. It is not available to the public.

What it accesses. With the read-only permissions youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly, it reads: the channel's name and ID, subscriber, view and video counts, the list of uploaded videos with their titles, status and view/like counts, and aggregate YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, subscribers gained and lost). It cannot upload, edit or delete anything.

How it is used. The data is used only to draw a progress dashboard for the channel owner. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with anyone, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Where it is stored. Only on the team's own computer: a sign-in token per channel, and the generated dashboard page. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.

Deleting it. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or via Google security settings; locally stored tokens and dashboard files are deleted on request or when the tool is no longer used.

Seven Lanterns Stats' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
