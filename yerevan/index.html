The place

Yerevan by night

Yerevan is the capital of Armenia and one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world. On a clear day, the snow-capped peaks of Mount Ararat rise above the city, so close they seem to float over the rooftops.

Many buildings are made of tuff, a volcanic stone in shades of pink, apricot and rose. At sunset the whole city glows warm, which is why it is often called the Pink City.

Around it lie mountains, monasteries and quiet places: the old temple of Garni above a deep gorge, the monastery of Geghard carved into the rock, and Lake Sevan, high up in the mountains, cold and clear.

The voice of this channel is the duduk, a double-reed instrument traditionally carved from apricot wood. Its sound is soft, warm and a little sad, often played over a steady low drone called the dam. In 2005 UNESCO recognised duduk music as a masterpiece of the world's intangible heritage. Let its long, slow notes guide your breathing tonight.
