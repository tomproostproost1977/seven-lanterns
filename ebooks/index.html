Sleep a little better in seven nights

A gentle, illustrated sleep ritual for one quiet week. Each evening has one short practice, one scene to picture and one hour of music. No apps, no pressure.

Inside the guide

One small step each evening

Nights 1–2

Arrive and let the day go

Slow your breathing with the long exhale, then put tomorrow’s worries on paper so they can wait until morning.

Nights 3–5

Come back into the body

A slow body scan, square breathing and a simple tense-and-release to find tension in the jaw, shoulders and hands, and let it go.

Nights 6–7

End on something good

Three good things from the day, then a final night of simply listening, with the lantern light growing still.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. You leave your email, we send the guide, and now and then a short letter when a new mix is out. One click unsubscribes you.

Do I need the music?

It helps, but no. Every night names a one-hour mix that is free to play on YouTube. Start the music, lower the volume, then do the practice.

Which city should I choose?

Pick the sound you like best: Persian ambient (Shiraz), Armenian duduk (Yerevan), flamenco guitar (Seville), Japanese zen (Kyoto), café jazz (Paris), Celtic harp (Galway) or Indian raga (Varanasi). The practices are the same in every guide.

Is this medical advice?

No. The guide is for relaxation and winding down. If you have ongoing trouble sleeping, please talk to a doctor or another health professional.

Can I share it?

Yes, please do. Share it unchanged with anyone who could use a calmer night. Selling it or passing it off as your own is not allowed.
