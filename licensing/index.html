Questions

Before you ask

How is the music made?

Our tracks are created with AI music tools on a paid commercial plan, then selected, arranged, sequenced and mastered by us. We license them to you under a written agreement. Read more on the about page.

Do I still pay a collecting society for this music?

Our recordings and compositions are not registered with SGAE, AGEDI, AIE or any other collecting society, so your licence comes directly from us. If you also play music by other artists, that music is licensed separately.

Will my video get a copyright claim?

It shouldn't. If a claim ever appears on a video covered by your licence, send us the link and we will clear it, usually the same day.

Can I use the music in several locations?

A Venue licence covers one physical location. For chains, franchises or several studios we make a single group price. Just ask.

What is not allowed?

Reselling or redistributing the tracks as music (for example as your own album or in a music library), registering them with Content ID or a collecting society, and using them to train AI models. Full terms are on the terms page.
