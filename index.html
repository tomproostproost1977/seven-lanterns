Three moods

A mix for every hour of the night

Sleep · 70 BPM

Drift off

Slow and instrumental, with nothing sudden. Made to fade into the background while you fall asleep.

Focus · 80 BPM

Stay with it

A steady, gentle pulse for reading, study and deep work, warm enough to keep you company for hours.

Voice · 75 BPM

Breathe out

A wordless female voice over the music, for meditation, yoga and slow evenings.

Every mix runs a full hour, with soft crossfades between tracks so nothing jolts you awake.
